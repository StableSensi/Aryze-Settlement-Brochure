02 · THE PROBLEM

The cost of building, or the cost of waiting.

Legacy core systems were never built to hold or settle digital assets.

Building that capability in-house is a multi-million, multi-year commitment.

Standing still is not free either: payments in digital assets are growing, and clients are finding faster, cheaper routes.

The mechanism behind it.

One incoming payment becomes an ordinary krone credit. These are the units it passes through on the way.
